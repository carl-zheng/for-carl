# Business Defaults

Apply these first-party advertiser policies to the user's current ecommerce product portfolio unless the user explicitly provides a product- or campaign-specific exception.

## United States defaults

- Standard shipping is free when the order subtotal reaches **$49**.
- The standard return period is **30 days**.

These are advertiser policies, not AppLovin rules or general ecommerce facts. Label them as Level B first-party inputs when evidence labels are useful.

## Advertising source and creative license

- Treat every claim, visual, offer, review, social-proof element, and other piece of content explicitly published on an advertiser-controlled product detail page as approved first-party advertising input. Label it `PAGE-CLEARED`.
- Preserve material qualifications and the page's intended meaning. Record a snapshot date or source URL, and recheck changing values such as price, compare-at price, review count, inventory, recent orders, timers, and discounts immediately before launch.
- Product-page customer quotations and social proof may be reused when copied faithfully. Do not fabricate a new customer, quotation, count, or event.
- Creative exaggeration is allowed when it is recognizable puffery: subjective, emotional, playful, or metaphorical language that a reasonable viewer would not treat as a measurable fact. Label it `PUFFERY` when claim auditing is useful.
- Do not use puffery to invent or alter objective facts such as quantity, dimensions, materials, compatibility, safety, health effects, certifications, guarantees, prices, discounts, shipping, returns, review counts, sales volume, scarcity, or performance. Those facts need either a product-page source or explicit user approval.

## Application rules

- Keep the threshold and return period consistent across video, AIP, landing page, cart, checkout, and supporting policy pages.
- A user-approved exception overrides the default only for the specified product or campaign; record the exception in the Product Truth.
- Do not infer the shipping charge below $49, the event that starts the 30-day period, return eligibility conditions, exclusions, or refund mechanics unless the user or an approved policy source supplies them.
- When a page conflicts with these defaults, treat the values above as the intended policy, flag the page for correction, and avoid inventing missing operational details.
